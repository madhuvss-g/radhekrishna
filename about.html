About

Small-scale manufacturer with a long, practical history.

Radhekrishna Enterprises is a small-scale industry that has been active since 1993, serving under different names but staying focused on manufacturing and supplying hardware.

Today, the company is the sole manufacturer of G-MEX friction hinges and also supplies a wider range of hardware materials, including items that are not limited to metals. The goal is simple: make it easy for clients to get the hinges and related hardware they actually need.

How the work has stayed consistent since 1993

The company’s history goes back to 1993. Over the years, it has served with different names, but the core work remained close to manufacturing and supplying real, everyday hardware.

That continuity means the current Radhekrishna Enterprises name carries experience from many years of serving clients with hinges and hardware, not a brand that appeared overnight.

  • • Active since 1993 as a small-scale industry
  • • Experience across different trading and business names
  • • Consistent focus on hinge and hardware supply work

What Radhekrishna Enterprises focuses on today

Today, the key focus is to manufacture G-MEX friction hinges and support that manufacturing with a practical hardware supply range.

That range is not limited to metals and can include hardware materials like plastic and other items that become part of the client’s project or product.
