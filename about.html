About us

Built on three decades of
real hardware work.

Radhekrishna Enterprises is a sole proprietorship based in Hyderabad, manufacturing and supplying hinges and hardware since 1993. Founded by Badarinath Gattupalli, the business has grown from a small trading operation into a manufacturer with a wide supply network across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

Our story

From 1993 to today.

1993
The beginning

Badarinath Gattupalli starts a small hardware trading and manufacturing business in Hyderabad. The focus from day one is on hinges and hardware for the construction and fabrication industry.

1993 – 2009
Sudarshan Enterprises

The business operates under the name Sudarshan Enterprises, building relationships with dealers, contractors and construction companies across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. The core work — manufacturing and supplying hinges — stays consistent throughout.

2009
Radhekrishna Enterprises

The business is re-registered as Radhekrishna Enterprises. The name carries the spirit of Lord Krishna — a name chosen with meaning and intention. The work continues without interruption.

2017
GST Registration

Radhekrishna Enterprises registers under GST in July 2017, formalising the business further and making it easier for corporate and government-linked clients to work with us.

Today
Manufacturing & supply, expanded

Today, Radhekrishna Enterprises manufactures pivot hinges, stay hinges and friction stays, and supplies a wide range of hardware on demand. Clients include NCL Buildtek, DEC Infra, Ahlada and many more dealers and contractors across India.
